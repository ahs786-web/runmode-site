RunMode — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

RunMode is a running and endurance-training app for iPhone. It is built around a simple principle: your training and health data stays on your device. This policy explains the few things that leave your device, who receives them, and why — in plain English.

The short version. Almost everything RunMode does happens on your iPhone. Your Apple Health data, workouts, routes, personal bests, notes and coach conversations are stored and processed on the device and are never sent to us — in fact, there is no RunMode server and we never see your data. A few optional features send a limited amount of data off your device: the AI coach (to Apple's private models on iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, otherwise to Google) and weather (to Apple and to Open-Meteo). We do not track you, run no analytics, show no ads, and never sell your data.

RunMode ("RunMode", "we", "us") is operated by RunMode Ltd, a data controller based in the United Kingdom.

We are the "controller" of the personal data described below for the purposes of the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. What stays on your device

When you grant RunMode permission to read Apple Health, the app reads and processes the following entirely on your iPhone. This data is not transmitted to us and there is no account or cloud sync:

  • Workouts and routes (runs, rides, swims, walks), heart rate and resting heart rate, and sleep;
  • Derived training information — readiness scores, training load, heart-rate zones, personal bests, and route history;
  • Anything you enter in the app — check-ins, symptoms, subjective feel notes, event/race plans, and your AI-coach conversations;
  • Your age, sex and weight, used only on-device to estimate heart-rate zones and guidance when you have not set values yourself.

Because this data lives on your device, you control it directly — deleting the app removes it, and coach conversations can be cleared inside the app at any time (Profile → clear conversations).

3. What leaves your device

There are exactly three features that send data off your device, each to a named third party, each only to make that feature work.

(a) The AI coach — Apple Intelligence or Google Gemini (optional)

The AI coach runs only if you use it — by opening a coach chat, or by receiving a coaching notification you enabled. When it runs, your question and the training context needed to answer it are processed by an AI model. Which model depends on your iPhone, and the coach follows the same coaching rules whichever engine answers. The context can include: your readiness score and what fed it, recent workout summaries (activity type, distance, duration, pace, heart-rate zones, training-load ratio), sleep hours, any symptoms or feel notes you logged, your target event, your age, and the messages you type. In every case we do not send your name, email address, precise location, or any account identifier.

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence (and unless you turn Apple Intelligence off under Profile → Coach), the coach uses Apple's models first: short coaching notifications are generated by Apple's on-device model (kept on the phone), and full coach chats use Apple Private Cloud Compute, Apple's privacy-preserving cloud. Apple states that data sent to Private Cloud Compute is not retained after your request is fulfilled and is not used to train Apple's models. If Apple's model is ever unavailable or declines a request (for example when a device usage limit is reached), the coach falls back to Google's Gemini API described below so it can still answer — and in that case the same context is sent to Google. Apple's handling is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, the coach uses Google's paid Gemini API. Under Google's terms for that tier, your prompts and responses are not used to train or improve Google's models; Google retains them only briefly for abuse-detection, safety and legal purposes. The data leaves your device to Google, so if you would prefer that nothing be sent to a third party, simply do not use the coach — every figure the coach uses is also shown in the app itself. Google's handling is governed by the Google Gemini API terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

(b) Current weather & race-day forecast — Apple WeatherKit

To show current conditions and your race-day forecast, RunMode sends your device's location to Apple WeatherKit. This is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy terms. See Apple's Privacy Policy and the Apple Weather data sources.

(c) Past-run weather — Open-Meteo

To show the weather a past run happened in, RunMode sends the run's approximate start location (rounded to about 1 km) and its date to Open-Meteo, a weather archive service. No account, identifier, or other data is sent. See the Open-Meteo terms.

4. Our lawful bases (UK GDPR)

Health and fitness data is "special category" data under the UK GDPR, which requires both a lawful basis (Article 6) and an additional condition (Article 9). Our bases are:

ActivityLawful basis (Art. 6)Special-category condition (Art. 9)
On-device processing of your Health dataConsent — you grant Apple Health access and choose to use the appExplicit consent
Processing coaching data (Apple Intelligence, or Google Gemini)Consent — the coach is optional; you choose to use itExplicit consent
Sending location for weatherConsent — you grant location access for the weather featuresNot applicable (location is not special-category data)

You can withdraw consent at any time by turning off the relevant feature, revoking permissions in iOS Settings, or deleting the app. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not operate a server or account system, and we never receive or store your data ourselves.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites (the app declares no tracking).
  • We use no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We never sell or rent your data.

6. International transfers

Apple, Google and Open-Meteo may process the limited data described above on servers outside the UK. Where they do, those transfers are covered by the providers' own safeguards (such as UK adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses), as set out in their respective terms linked above.

7. How long data is kept

  • On your device: for as long as the app is installed. You can delete it at any time, and coach conversations can be cleared in-app.
  • Google / Apple / Open-Meteo: according to their own retention terms (for Gemini's paid tier, brief retention for abuse, safety and legal purposes only, as described above).

8. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because almost all of your data is held on your own device, you can exercise most of these rights directly — you have access to it in the app, and deleting the app or clearing conversations erases it. For anything you cannot do yourself, contact us at info@runmode.app.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, though we would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

9. Children

RunMode is a general-audience fitness app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly process the data of children under 16.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.
