4. Health and safety: training guidance only, not medical advice

4.1 RunMode gives training information and guidance. It is not a medical device and it is not medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any illness or injury, and it is no substitute for a doctor or another qualified professional.

4.2 Heart rate, heart-rate zones, readiness, training load, distance, pace, calorie estimates and other figures come from sensors, from Apple Health and from estimates. They can be wrong.

4.3 Talk to a doctor before you start or change your training if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, take medicine that affects your heart rate, or are coming back from illness or injury. Stop and get medical help if you have chest pain, feel faint or dizzy, or are unusually short of breath.

4.4 RunMode is not designed for emergencies or for life-saving use. Don't rely on it to keep you safe or to get help.

4.5 Stay aware of your surroundings. Watch the traffic and the ground, not the screen. Follow traffic laws and road signs, and don't run anywhere that is unsafe or where you have no right to be.

5. The AI coach and imported plans

5.1 The AI coach is optional. Nothing is sent to it until you allow it, and you can turn it off in the app at any time. Its replies are written by Google's Gemini, an AI model, using your question and the training data our privacy policy describes.

5.2 AI replies can be wrong, incomplete or out of date, even when they sound confident. Treat them as training guidance only, not medical advice, and check anything important before you act on it.

5.3 RunMode also uses AI to read a training plan you import: Google's Gemini if you have allowed the AI coach, otherwise Apple's on-device model on iPhones that have it. Either can misread a plan, for example a distance or the number of repeats, so check every session before you save it.

6. Weather, location and routes

6.1 Current weather, forecasts and weather alerts in RunMode come from Apple Weather (data sources: https://developer.apple.com/weatherkit/data-source-attribution/). Heat guidance, run windows and other advice based on the weather are estimates. Weather can change quickly, and it may differ from the forecast or from the conditions where you are.

6.2 GPS and other location data can be wrong, for example near tall buildings, under trees, in tunnels or when the signal is weak. Distances, paces, maps, the routes you follow and turn cues may be wrong as a result. Always use your own judgement, and follow road signs and the law rather than anything RunMode tells you.

6.3 Apple requires us to include these two notices:

These notices are a warning to use your own judgement. They do not limit what section 10 says we are responsible for, and they do not take away your legal rights.
