The problem

Running apps make things up. You can't tell which bits.

A recovery score with no visible inputs. A readiness number from nowhere. An AI summary that sounds sure about data it doesn't have. If you can't see what's measured and what's guessed, you can't trust any of it.

What you usually get

  • One score, no working shown
  • Gaps filled with something that looks right
  • A plan you're expected to follow

What RunMode does

  • Scores that open up into the parts that made them
  • "—" when the inputs aren't good enough
  • Coaching off your real week, plan-free

Closed apps are a black box. AI chat coaches fly blind. RunMode has your real data, explains every decision — and you can check the maths.

How it works

Three steps. No questionnaire.

  1. Connect Apple Health

    RunMode reads the runs, heart rate and sleep already on your phone and watch.

  2. Add your race

    Date, distance, goal. Everything gets anchored to the race you're actually training for.

  3. Check the working

    Tap a score to see what fed it. Ask the coach why. The answer points back at your data.

Questions

Straight answers

Can I download it?

Not yet. There's no public beta and no App Store listing. Leave your email and you'll hear when that changes.

When does it open?

There's no date. It opens once a few privacy and App Store compliance items are done. We'd rather say that than make one up.

What will it cost?

That isn't decided, so we're not putting a number here.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

RunMode reads Apple Health, so it works with whatever writes there. A watch gives it heart rate and sleep to work with. Without those, the numbers that need them show "—" until there's real data behind them.

Does it work with Garmin, Coros or other watches?

Only through Apple Health. If your watch's app syncs runs into Apple Health, RunMode can read them. That hasn't been tested across brands yet, so we won't promise it.

Will it give me a training plan?

No, on purpose. It coaches off what's actually happening in your training and your life, not a fixed schedule it has to defend.

What leaves my phone?

When you use the coach, the training context it needs and your messages go to Google's Gemini API to write the reply. Your location goes to Apple WeatherKit for forecasts, and a rough run location (about 1 km) and date go to Open-Meteo for past weather. No analytics, ads or tracking. The privacy policy has the full list.

Who's building it?

One runner, through RunMode Ltd in the UK. Every feature gets tested on his own runs first.
